FOOT BALL

V izag is notorious for not having much space but sports is where the city seems to make an exception. In the last few years, the popularity of ‘turf football’ has spiked dramatically and at ASC, we are committed to ensuring that all of our members constantly have access to the best.
ASC-5-A-SIDE provides two football pitches designed to maximize your pleasure playing “The Beautiful Game”. The turf is developed with international level infrastructure and is a stellar platform to develop amateur and casual football in India. Special rates for ASC always help!
With gruelling school and work schedules, social commitments, traffic and a host of other things taking up your precious time, we want to cut through the unpredictability to offer you something you look forward to and can rely on. Just make a booking online at www.urbansports.in and you’re good to go!
Members are encouraged to appropriately – turf shoes and rubber studs are permitted while steel studs are not. Footballs and bibs (when requested) are provided as part of the package, though refreshments are chargeable.
NOTE: Members are free to organize tournaments and avail of coaching classes by Soccer Schools of Excellence (SSE) and Premier India Football Academy (PIFA).
