SQUASH

T he ASC squash program was and continues to be one of its shining lights. The club houses four regulation courts so that results are comparable and members who have played at the venue more do not benefit from an unfair advantage. Each room is air-conditioned and sculpted to international standards. With one doubles court and three singles courts, members are treated daily to best in class facilities.
Host to matches from Professional Squash Association (PSA) tournaments, ASC Open, separate home tournaments for members and Inter-Club tournaments as well, ASC is the preferred location for the aspiring amateur or seasoned professional.
The first come first serve system works perfectly to ensure that none of our members are left out. Thirty (30) minute sessions are in order on days when there is a lot of footfall, whereas members can feel free to play to their hearts content when that is not the case.
We also have markers on the premises – ever-present, knowledgeable and proficient, they provide the perfect sounding board for members that come alone but want to avail of the squash court. ASC also provides coaching three times a week, with three different coaches. Members wishing to avail of coaching services are encouraged to make inquiries at the sign-in desk.
NOTE: Members are expected to bring their own personal rackets and play in gum-sole shoes only. The club provides balls.
NOTE: We even have a pro-shop (NAS) right outside for our members to avail of discounted prices on maintenance, repair and purchase of sporting equipment across badminton, squash, tennis, table tennis, swimming, jogging and football.
