HOUSIE CLUB

O ne of ASC members’ favourite pastimes, Housie is a club classic. Hosted every Wednesday and Saturday from 6:00pm onwards on the East Lawns, we have 10 games per session to ensure you’re doing more than feeling lucky. With fantastic prizes, guests permitted and a yearly mega event with giveaways including the finest material things money can buy (or in this case, win, with a little luck), there is absolutely no reason why you shouldn’t come and join in the fun!
