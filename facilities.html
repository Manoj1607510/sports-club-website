FACILITIES

We go the extra mile to make your time at the club everything you want it to be. We offer you intellectual stimulation at the Book Nook, entertainment in the theater and Dome arena and relaxation at the Saloon or Siesta Room.
But most of all, we want to make your life easier by adding more conveniences. This is why we have dedicated child-friendly sections to keep the tiny tots as well as the not-so-youngsters busy, as you explore all that the various activities, experiences and facilities the club has to offer. We even offer a full-fledged grocery store and cut-price, top quality accommodation. Go on, enjoy yourself. We take a lot of pride and joy in ensuring that you do.
ASC has been the breeding ground for countless athletes in their pursuit for glory. Today, we are proud to continue that rich tradition but sparing no effort in bringing world class facilities, infrastructure and coaching to our members on the same grounds and courts that national level tournaments are played on. With numerous BADMINTON, TENNIS and squash courts of international size and standards, facilities for outdoor sports like CRICKET and FOOTBALL and a swimming pool that is always a hotbed of activity, RSC offers something special for all of our athletically inclined patrons. Not to be left behind in the indoor gaming department, the club also houses Billiards, Chess, Carrom and Table Tennis facilities.
