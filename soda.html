SODA FOUNTAIN

S oda Fountain provides your daily dose of healthy eating and refreshments, served up fresh and quick at any time of the day. Get the nourishment you need with freshly squeezed juices directly from nature’s lap to your stomach. Andhra’s sweltering heat can be quite unforgiving. The dirt, grime, sweat and smoldering temperature can be dehydrating and zap your energy. In such times, all you need is a chilled glass of your favourite fruit juice. Whether you are taking your first steps towards healthier living or continuing on a path to a sustainable lifestyle, we’ve got you covered. Stop by to quench your thirst with healthy coconut water, rich in nutrition and strength to go with your active persona. Give your body the essential vitamins and nutrients it needs. We also provide fresh-cut seasonal fruit platters to give you the benefits of more fibre and multi-vitamins. Nothing can replace the taste and health benefits of fruit in its natural form. Order yours today, you’ll thank yourself later. If that’s not your style, we also have aerated drinks and draft beers depending on your mood or hydration requirements.
