BILLIARDS

T he new billiards and snooker room was christened “The Baulk Line” at the opening ceremony which was on the 4th January 2014.
Situated at the 1st floor of the new stadium building (D block entrance) it truly marks a blend of fine architectural design with superb English styled interiors. Spread over a carpet area of 4000 sq. ft. it hosts 6 full length snooker and billiard tables and also 2 international pool tables.
With a full service pantry and 10 well trained markers and a fully equipped billiards room, it truly boasts of being one of the best available venues for the sport across the country which can be enjoyed by ASC members.
The Baulk Line has been witness to various national tournaments from its inception and offers the opportunity or club members to only see the best in India perform but also interact with them to further improve on their games.
The tournaments held at The Baulk Line has also witnessed all-time great Mr. Yasin Mechant (Asian snooker champion) playing at the venue and also mentioning that “The Baulk Line” is one of the best snooker and billiards room he has ever played at.
The Baulk Line is open 7 days a week with regular timing from 2:00 pm till 10.00 pm and on Saturdays or other bank holidays from 11:30 am till 10.00 pm and Sunday 11.00 am till 10.00 pm to with a mere monthly charge of Rs. 900/- and a yearly charge of Rs. 8,500. Half-hour per session charges of Rs. 20 make it a very affordable and enjoyable experience to the ASC members.
