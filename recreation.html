RECREATION

Never content to rest on our laurels as a Sporting icon in the country, at the ASC, we go far beyond in our endeavor to provide the most wholesome and enjoyable experiences to our members. Beyond a plethora of restaurants, banquet halls and conveniences ranging from supermarkets to libraries, we also bring you outdoor movies and gala housie nights! While the kids hone their karate or skating skills, older members can pursue physical development in our state-of-the-art fitness center or hone their mental fortitude in leisure at the dedicated card room, Vegas.
