VEGAS


V egas is our in-house card room for you to hone or exhibit those Rummy skills of yours. We have 30 tables, each of which seat anywhere from 4-8 people, as your game requires. The perfect way to spend time with your friends or fellow club patrons and Rummy enthusiasts, any time spent in Vegas is time well spent. We provide the chips, we provide the cards and we provide snacks from a kitchen dedicated solely for Vegas players. Members are allowed to sign in guests, though all card room participants are required to be a minimum of 18 years of age.
