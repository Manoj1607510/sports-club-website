FOODS AND DRINKS AT ASC

At ASC, we like to make sure that you’re always taken care of. With unmatched variety on offer, there is something for everyone and every mood. No matter where you are in the Club, you are never too far from great food and a comfortable area to enjoy some refreshments. We have palatial fine dining for special occasions, multi-cuisine restaurants to satisfy every taste, a coffee shop and confectionery, convenient healthy fast food, ice-cream parlous and juice and fruit options available throughout the day. So whether you’re here to take a breather between activities, catch up with friends, spend quality time with family, host a business lunch, unwind after a long day’s work or even party the night way on weekends – we have you covered every step of the way!
