LIBRARY

A ptly named the “Book Nook”, the library is open from the early morning hours until just past dinner time. Regardless of the time of day, if you’re looking for some peace and quiet, tranquil places to study, the right ambiance to catch up on work or just the right retreat to catch up on all the reading you’ve missed out on in your hectic digital life, come on in. As our lives become more active, the library gives you an opportunity to step away from it all for a little while. Seating 30+ where you enter and more upstairs, the Book Nook includes a wide and eclectic collection of information on various topics in several languages. The ample seating pace provides abundant and comfortable seating to patrons who want to read the bountiful daily newspapers, books and magazines that stock this room.
