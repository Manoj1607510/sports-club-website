ROYAL IMPERIAL LOUNGE

R oyal Imperial Lounge is the newest eatery at the club and is an ideal fit for social gatherings. Located at the hub of RSC’s recreational offerings, its exotic ambience and décor with walls adorned by colourful paintings makes for a vivacious atmosphere. Its perfect lighting and the soothing music blends well with the beautiful surroundings, a perfect respite for you when you’re done at the Gym or the theatre. Our select ‘a la Carte’ menu offers a variety of refreshments and assorted snacking options amid the aromatic vibes courtesy of our muffins and confectionery items. Enjoy the finest in club cuisine and don’t forget to inquire about daily soup and salad specials and some of our special delights – freshly prepared pizzas, pastas, wraps and omelettes. The vegetarian alakiev and batada vadas are a particular crowd favourite. Perfect for business meetings, Royal Imperial Lounge’s spacious 124 seater space means you can always find your own nook to spend quality time with whomever your party includes. Please make a note that after 6:00pm, patrons are expected to be in full pants and close-toed shoes.
