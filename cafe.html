ROUND THE CORNER(CAFE)

W ith a capacity of up to 60 people, Round The Corner offers you refreshments ranging from sandwiches and soups to chaat and South Indian cuisine depending on the time of day you stop by. A coffee shop at heart with comfortable seating that wouldn’t look out of place at home, the layout and ambiance makes this the ideal place for business meetings, casual conversations over a tasty brew, a calm environment to catch up on work or to simply read and catch up on some well-deserved ‘me’ time. In the world of on-demand programming, keeping you connected is something we take seriously so you never have to miss live developments in the realm of News & Sports. Whether you’d like to catch up on how the markets are doing, or soak in some IPL or Football fun, we’ve got you covered. Round The Corner is designed to suit your convenience. People flock here for the coffee, the relaxed atmosphere, as a stop between activities and every other reason you can and cannot yet think of.
