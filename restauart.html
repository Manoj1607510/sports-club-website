RESTAURANT

W ith 32 tables and a seating capacity of 135-155, Excellency is always a good choice. Home to a multi-cuisine restaurant specializing in Chinese, Indian and a substantial amount of Continental fare, club-catering has never been better. We offer the choicest liquors, the finest wines and the tastiest cocktails mixed by our experienced bartenders. Excellency has a well-stocked bar that offers a wide range of drinks, served with a smile at very reasonable rates. Whether you’re in a small or large group, we’ve got you covered. Not too loud, not too soft, the friendly drum of patrons enjoying themselves makes Excellency the perfect place to unwind after a tough day at work. It’s also a great place to enjoy taking in the latest in live sports or news with plenty of TVs and even a Projector. Your seat always has a tremendous viewing angle. Best enjoyed on Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights, Excellency also turns up the dial and the music to host occasional DJ nights for people to dust off the week that was, shake a leg and dance!
