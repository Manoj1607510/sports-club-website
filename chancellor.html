CHANCELLOR


I f you prefer an indoor function, ASC’s Chancellor Hall works well as an out-room for close family and friends during a wedding function and even better for small groups and family get-togethers. Almost tailor-made for the intimate special occasion with a select and private guest list, you cannot find anything better than the Chancellor. With a capacity of 15-30 people, it comes fully equipped with DJ-friendly points as well as a big projector. So regardless of whether your needs are for a corporate presentation, baby shower, wedding function, video screening, birthday or just an intimate meal with the family, Chancellor is the ideal choice. Simple but stylish and with disproportionately expansive menu options thanks to the never-ending F&B options at the club, Chancellor strikes just about every note you could want it to.
