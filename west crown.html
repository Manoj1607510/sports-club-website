WEST CROWN


The well designed hall can transform an adequate boardroom with modern conferencing facilities to a well-defined area for banqueting. With a high ceiling, an elevated wooden stage and an advanced sound system, your occasion will be one of sophistication and style. Whether you are planning a wedding reception, a corporate function or an elegant sit-down dinner, West Crown makes the planning easy. We excel in accommodating the unique needs of each and every client, from a small business meeting to a banquet serving up to 1,000 people. Adorable décor and flower services and delicious spread of food is provided by a group of selected decorators and caterers.
