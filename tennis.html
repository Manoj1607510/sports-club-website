TENNIS

W ith six official regulation courts, ASC provides one of the city’s most comfortable and professional tennis playing experiences. Renovated as recently as 2016, four courts are tucked away near the swimming pool area in the Old Wing and come complete with all the amenities you can ask for, even flood lights for the waning visibility during the sunset hours. They also benefit from the proximity to the swimming pool and patrons can avail of snacks from the poolside pantry and refreshments on site (chargeable). Two more courts are located near the club entrance in the event the courts are occupied or if club members want to play in a location more conducive to sea breeze and a view. The rubber courts have material imported from the United States of America and have a uniformity and quality one can expect from the club, undoubtedly one of the chief reasons the club has and will continue to host National-level tournaments. Members are requested to be appropriately attired, with tennis shoes. The club provides rackets and balls for member’s convenience. Tennis coaching is also offered in the evenings and interested parties are encouraged to inquire with the club about the same.
