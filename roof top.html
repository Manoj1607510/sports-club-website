ROOF TOP UPPER TERRACE


S ituated right next to Chambers, the beautiful Roof Top Lower Terrace is a flight of stairs away from the larger capacity Upper Terrace but offers some of the same benefits. Coupled with the soothing sea breeze and the serene sound of the waves in between the lilted melodies of laughter and music from your get together, the Lower Terrace plays well with a variety of events. With special club-catering and multiple cuisines for vegetarians and non-vegetarians, all of your guests are covered no matter their preferences. All the equipment, liquor permits, etc. you need is made available for you. Add-ons are chargeable at the lowest rates for our patrons and are only a simple request away. All you have to do is say the word, and our professional staff will take care of your needs with the speed and efficiency to put your event organizational worries to rest. No matter the occasion, we strive tirelessly to ensure that you and your guests will always have a memorable time.
