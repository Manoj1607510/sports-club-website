VICEROY

T he Viceroy is best known for its excellent ambiance. It’s muted lighting, intimate seating designed to best enjoy the interactive convenience and authentic sumptuous ‘a la Carte’ menu, full with its creative twists makes it a happening space to hang out! Catered by one of Andhra’s finest, the 108 seater family restaurant is perfect for any occasion. Host to special theme nights from ghazal to karaoke, there’s a lot more to this regal eatery than meets the eye. Viceroy is a place you and the family can relax and socialize at the same time. In addition to its vast range of authentic Italian cuisine, you can also enjoy delectable continental food, Lebanese, Indian and Chinese as well. Make sure you catch the scrumptious Italian pastas and pizzas apart from other exotic dishes.
