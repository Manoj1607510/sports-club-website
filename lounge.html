A LOUNGE


T he perfect location for all your special occasions in life, the Emperor Hall is one of the finest luxury banquet halls in the heart of Andhra. It is a mix of modern elegance and royal charm befitting of any celebratory event. It is both at once – equal parts regal and warm, with an excellent layout and provides a picture-perfect ambience. Ideal for birthdays, anniversaries and specially wedding functions, the Emperor Hall is an exquisite venue that comes with expansive multi-cuisine catering menus from around the club, certain to send your guests and loved ones home with a satisfied smile on their faces. Despite the bevy of amenities, food and beverage around you, we ensure they and the staff are not intrusive so that the focus remains on your centre of attention. We have played host to everything from Corporate functions to Sangeets. Whether you’re looking for a classy affair, revelry for the ages or both at the same time, the Emperor Halls welcomes you.
