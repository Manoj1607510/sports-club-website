TABLE TENNIS

T able tennis has been growing both as a sport and as a pleasurable way to spend some time, while developing your skills and hand-eye coordination. The sport is popular because of its easy to understand rules and simplicity – making it a fantastic activity for beginners and experienced players alike. At ASC, the Table Tennis Room caters to players of all ages and skill levels. Our markers are always available so you always have a partner even if you’re alone. Interested parties can also place inquiries for coaching facilities (minimum age 6 | maximum batch size of 12). The skilled coaches can assist beginners in learning the sport, and also develop the skills of experienced players. With 4 regulation size world-class centrefold tables, sport appropriate flooring, ample spacing, high ceilings and bright concentration lighting, the ASC Table Tennis Room is an air-conditioned sporting breeding ground that plays host to numerous tournaments including World Championship and Maharashtra State Ranking events. Equipment: The club provides balls and rackets free of charge. Members and guests are required to wear sports clothing and shoes to avail this facility. NOTE: We even have a pro-shop (NAS) right outside for our members to avail of discounted prices on maintenance, repair and purchase of sporting equipment across badminton, squash, tennis, table tennis, swimming, jogging and football.
