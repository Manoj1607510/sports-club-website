CENTER STAGE

A .trip to the theater has its benefits – the feeling of anticipation, tubs of popcorn and of course, that all important big screen. However, when it comes to a better and more comfortable cinematic experience, Center Stage gives you everything you could ask for. With an abundance of parking at the club, cheaper ticket prices, a plethora of food options, a more intimate theater size and top notch sound – the movie experience is more comfortable, more economical and more fun. The modern 214 seater hall has regular screenings of some of the latest movies over Friday-Sunday just the way you like it – guaranteed seating and no waiting lines. You can also indulge yourself with group bookings, where the movie hall can be rented out on request. Choose between a massive selection of movies, both old and new when you opt for a private screening – a stellar option for birthday parties, client and employee appreciation events, group meetings and school field trips. Patrons can call the club and/or sign up for text messages for inquiries and information about movie selections and timings.
